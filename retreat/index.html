The Experience

Not escape. Recalibration.

Structure that creates freedom. A week of embodied balance — where intensity and stillness alternate by design, and every element is given its place.

Air

Morning and evening meditation anchor each day. Space to observe, breathe, and return to clarity before the next wave of engagement.

Earth

Hiking, outdoor practice, and coaching give form to insight. Structure you can feel in the body — not just understand in the mind.

Fire

Two martial arts sessions a day. Heat, discipline, and the direct confrontation with limits that only physical training can provide.

Water

Yin yoga closes each day. Integration time between sessions allows what was activated to settle, soften, and be absorbed.

Spirit

Shared meals, shared training, shared silence. Community not as an idea, but as daily practice — the element that emerges when everything else aligns.

Who it's for

People ready to do the work

This retreat is for people who want structure, not spectacle. Who are willing to train, reflect, and live in shared rhythm with others. Who understand that growth asks something of you — early mornings, honest effort, and the discipline to stay present when it would be easier not to. If that resonates, you belong here.
